Research Assistant

AI Speculative Bubbles & Multi-Scale Market Dynamics

Research conducted at TREE UMR CNRS 6031. Studied market dynamics (Microsoft, Tesla, Nasdaq) via EMD and Wavelet methods, isolating 80% of return energy in short-term fluctuations. Detected speculative bubbles using GSADF/BSADF tests (identifying Tesla's explosive phase with a test statistic of 2.965 vs threshold of 1.894). Developed Random Forest predictive models mapping behavioral indices (Google Trends, Wikipedia), achieving an R² of 0.65 for the Nasdaq index.
